Free to visit. Cheap to join. Quiet on purpose.

A good book, a warm chair, and nobody rushing you.

Readers’ Haven has served the city for more than sixty years. We lend over 48,000 books, keep the reading hall open six days a week, and still stamp due dates by hand if you ask nicely.

Our mission

We believe a library is the one place in a city where everyone is welcome and nobody has to buy anything. Our job is simple: keep good books within reach, give students a quiet place to work, and help people of every age find their way around information — on paper and online.

The library is run by the city with support from our Friends of the Library volunteers, who organise the Saturday story hour, mend old books and run the yearly book sale in March.

Exam season seats

Study rooms fill up fast in May and December. Book online up to 7 days ahead.

Book donations

Where we are

27 Canal View Road, opposite the old fire station, Lahore. Bus routes 12 and 41 stop right outside. Bicycle stands are behind the building and there is limited car parking on the east side.
