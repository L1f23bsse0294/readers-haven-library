Digital Services

The library, beyond the shelves

Search the catalogue from home, borrow e-books on your phone, or come in to print, scan and get online. Most services are free with any membership.

Online catalogue

Check whether a book is in, where it’s shelved, and place a hold before you leave the house.

  1. Go to the catalogue and type a title, author or ISBN.
  2. Click Place hold and sign in with your card number.
  3. We text you when it’s ready. Pick it up within 3 days.

Available 24 hours

E-books & audiobooks

Around 6,000 e-books and 900 audiobooks in English and Urdu, readable on any phone, tablet or laptop.

  1. Download the library reading app.
  2. Log in with your card number and PIN.
  3. Borrow up to 3 titles for 14 days. They return themselves.

Available 24 hours · Adult, Family & Senior cards

Study-room booking

Eight small rooms on the first floor, each with a table for four, a whiteboard and a power strip.

  1. Book online or at the front desk, up to 7 days ahead.
  2. Rooms are held for 15 minutes after your start time.
  3. Maximum 2 hours per member per day.

Mon–Thu 9 am – 7:30 pm · Sat 10 am – 5:30 pm

Free Wi-Fi

Fast Wi-Fi across both floors and the garden reading area.

  1. Connect to the network Haven-Public.
  2. Enter your card number, or ask the desk for a 1-day guest code.
  3. Sessions last 3 hours and can be renewed.

During opening hours

Not sure how to use something? Our staff run a free “Digital basics” class every Tuesday at 4 pm — email, online forms and the e-book app.
