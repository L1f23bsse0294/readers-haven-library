Membership

Pick a card that fits you

Anyone can walk in and read for free. A membership lets you take books home, reserve study rooms and use our e-book app. Bring your CNIC (or B-form for children) and one passport-size photo to the front desk.

Late returns & lost books

  1. You get a 2-day grace period after the due date. No fine is charged in those two days.
  2. After the grace period, the daily late fee in the table above applies to each book.
  3. Fines stop at PKR 500 per book, however late it is.
  4. If you owe more than PKR 300, borrowing is paused until the amount is paid.
  5. A book more than 45 days late is treated as lost. You pay the cover price plus a PKR 150 processing fee.
  6. Damaged books are assessed by the front desk; small repairs are free.
